Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

FinAccord Advisory is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data when you interact with our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any details you share when booking a consultation or contacting us via WhatsApp or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, schedule consultations, deliver our services, and — where you have opted in — send our monthly FinAccord Briefing newsletter. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Consultation bookings are stored securely via Supabase. We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by applicable law.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at advisory@finaccord.com.

5. Cookies

This website uses minimal browser storage (localStorage) only to remember your language preference. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please email us at advisory@finaccord.com.
