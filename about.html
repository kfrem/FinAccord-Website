About FinAccord

A New Standard in Financial Leadership for Africa

We are a collective of internationally qualified professionals who believe every ambitious African business deserves access to world-class financial intelligence and strategic guidance.

Our Story

FinAccord Advisory was born from a simple observation: Africa’s most ambitious companies are making million-dollar decisions on incomplete data. Not because they lack drive — but because they lack the financial visibility that mature organisations take for granted. We set out to change that.

Our founders are a group of Fellow Chartered Accountants spanning Europe and Africa, CPA members, senior data analysts, and corporate business coaches — with over 100 years of extensive operations across finance, business growth, and mentoring. We have built and scaled enterprises, restructured failing operations, coached founders through critical transitions, and delivered financial intelligence to organisations ranging from agricultural cooperatives in West Africa to technology ventures in London and Accra.

What sets us apart is the breadth of what we bring to the table. We don’t just balance the books — we see the whole picture. Our team has sat in boardrooms negotiating with investors, stood on factory floors understanding production costs, trained finance teams from scratch, and coached CEOs through the most difficult growth decisions of their careers. This is not theoretical consulting. Every recommendation we make is grounded in decades of hands-on operational experience.

We’ve combined this depth of experience with a proprietary financial intelligence system — built on Python, SQL, and advanced modelling frameworks — that transforms raw accounting chaos into board-ready intelligence in days, not weeks. The result is a service that no single hire could replicate: chartered-level financial rigour, analytical horsepower, and strategic business coaching, all delivered as a flexible, fractional engagement.
